# It's Just a Spank? What the Research Says

An interactive guide for parents who aren't convinced, covering what 50 years of research shows about spanking: brain, stress biology, emotions, attachment, behavior, learning, moral development, adult outcomes, and the next generation.

Available in English, Spanish, and Portuguese (`?lang=en`, `?lang=es`, `?lang=pt`).

Single static file: `index.html`. No build step. Sources are listed at the bottom of the page.

Compiled by Chris Wise. Last reviewed October 2026.
